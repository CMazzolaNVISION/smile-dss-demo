DSS Prototype

The question
How is this participant doing in the SMILE game?
What it uses
What they played, the points of the minigames chosen by clinical experts, and their mood just before and after playing.
How it answers
From the big picture to the detail: how far they got, how each module went, their results by skill, then every minigame.
How to read it
Points = share of the points possible. Mood before vs after shows what goes together with playing, not what it causes.

Game Performance

Participant: Lena Demo (Germany)
ⓘ About this view
How the participant plays the SMILE game, in five tabs: how far they have gone (Game progress), how each module went (Progression across modules), the results by subgroup of Healthy Thoughts and Healthy Behaviours, every minigame with its points and the mood around it (Minigame scores), and the interactions with the game's characters (Other metrics).

WHAT COUNTS: Game progress and Game completion count every part of the game (all its checkpoints). Points are awarded only in the minigames selected by clinical experts ("SMILE game scores and ESM relationship"), each with its own points rule.

DEFINITIONS:

- Points: points awarded / points possible, computed with the minigame's rule. Only the first completion of an exercise awards points; replays report the same value. Percentages only count minigames with points logged.

- Attempts (hover in Results by subgroup and Minigame scores): number of times the minigame's tasks were played, replays included.

- Mood (ESM): on the -3 to +3 scale, as everywhere in the DSS: -3 = negative mood, +3 = positive mood. Overall mood is the mean of "happy" and reversed "sad"; anxious and insecure are REVERSED (4 - answer, so +3 = not anxious, not insecure at all). For a minigame, the observation closest before and after it; for a module, the closest before its first minigame and after its last one; always within 2 days.

Game Progress

ⓘ About this view
How far into the game the participant has advanced over time (x = the date the game was played), grouped by module rather than raw checkpoint numbers, since checkpoint IDs are not evenly spaced and don't mean anything read on their own.

Game Completion

ⓘ About this view
Overall share of the game played so far, broken down by module. If the participant reached a module's final checkpoint, the whole module counts as completed even where some of its intermediate checkpoints were never directly logged.

Progression across modules

Modules compared metric by metric: as bar charts (one bar per module, in the module colours of Game progress) or as a scorecard (one cell per module).

ⓘ About this view
Points: share of the points possible awarded, in total and in Healthy Thoughts and Healthy Behaviours, counting only the minigames selected by clinical experts. ESM: overall mood, anxious (reversed) and insecure (reversed), on −3 to +3 (−3 = negative mood, +3 = positive mood), just before the module's first minigame and just after its last one (within 2 days), and the change (+ = towards positive mood). The grey line on each bar is the average of most participants for that module (700 participants in the cohort summary). A cross-hatched "none" stub means the module has no minigame of that kind; a hatched "n/a" stub means there is no data yet: neither is a 0. Scorecard view: the same results cell by cell, in two blocks: points from light grey (0%) to dark grey (100%); mood changes towards positive (blue) or negative (orange).
Pointsare awarded only in the minigames selected by clinical experts (not in every part of the game). Mood is on −3 to +3: −3 = negative mood, +3 = positive mood; anxious and insecure are reversed (+3 = not anxious, not insecure at all).

Every minigame selected by clinical experts, module by module. Hover a row for when it was played and the number of attempts.

ⓘ About this view
Task and activity: the colour of the text tells the category (Healthy Thoughts or Healthy Behaviours). Points: points awarded / points possible, with the share as a bar; only the first completion of an exercise awards points. A faded row has no score yet; the Points column says why (not played yet, played but no points logged by the game, or no scoring rule yet). Mood before / after: the ESM observation closest before and after the minigame (within 2 days), on −3 (negative mood) to +3 (positive mood): overall mood, anxious (reversed) and insecure (reversed); orange = negative, blue = positive. Change: ▲ towards positive mood, ▼ towards negative mood, = no change, – no observation before and after.
