DSS Prototype

The question
How is the SMILE cohort doing?
What it uses
Every participant's mood from the app, their country, gender and age, and how far they got in the game.
How it answers
First who is in the cohort, then how mood differs between countries and groups, then how far participants get in the game.
How to read it
Simple averages. Mood goes from −3 (negative, orange) to +3 (positive, blue). The selected participant is highlighted.

Cohort Overview

Participant: Lena Demo (Germany)
ⓘ About this view
Who is in the cohort, how a mood varies across countries and demographics, and how the cohort engages with the game. One tab per question.

COHORT DESCRIPTION: participants, countries, gender and age groups.

MOOD EXPLORATION ACROSS THE COHORT and DISTRIBUTION BY COUNTRY: pick a mood (a mood item or the overall mood) with the selector at the top of the tab; the map of Europe, the tree map and the distribution all show that mood.

MOOD SCALE: every mood is on a -3 to +3 scale (answers 1-7): + = better, - = worse, 0 = the middle of the scale. Negative moods (anxious, irritated, sad, insecure, lonely, stressed) are reversed, so higher is always better: -3 on "anxious" means very anxious, +3 not anxious at all.

GAME ENGAGEMENT: completion module by module, over the participants who started the game.

The selected participant is highlighted in the distribution and their country is outlined on the map.

Across Europe

What is the average relaxed in each country of the cohort?
ⓘ About this view
Each participating country is coloured by the cohort's average of the selected mood: orange = negative mood, grey = neutral, blue = positive mood. Countries not in the study have no fill.

SUGGESTED INTERACTION:

- Hover on a country for its average, standard deviation and number of participants (by gender).

- The selected participant's country has a thicker outline.

How is the cohort composed?

Size is proportional to the share of participants. Colour shows the average relaxed.
ⓘ About this view
HOW IS THE COHORT COMPOSED?

Shows participant distribution across demographic subgroups: country, then gender, then age group. Each box represents a subgroup (e.g., Italian females aged 10–17); its size is proportional to the share of participants.


SUGGESTED INTERACTION:

- Hover on boxes to explore information of each subgroup

- Click on boxes to focus on one subgroup

- Click on the little camera at top right of the plot card to download the plot as .PNG file


ADDITIONAL VISUAL ELEMENTS:

- Colour of boxes and colour bar: the subgroup's average of the mood picked in the dashboard, orange = negative mood, blue = positive mood; around 0 the box is transparent.
−3 = negative mood, +3 = positive mood.

Distribution by country: relaxed

ⓘ About this view
DISTRIBUTION BY COUNTRY:

One violin per country with every participant's average of the selected mood; the violin is coloured by the country's average (same colours as the map). Inside: a box (median and quartiles) and the mean (line). Each dot is a participant (shape and colour = gender); the large dot is the selected participant (blue = above 0, orange = below 0).

The lower half (below 0) is negative mood and the upper half positive mood; negative moods are reversed.

SUGGESTED INTERACTION:

- Hover on a violin for the country's mean, median and N; on a dot for the participant's gender, age group and value.

- Click a gender in the legend to hide or show it.

Completion by module

ⓘ About this view
The game over the cohort, module by module. A module here means the sublevels of that module the DSS analyses, chosen by the clinicians, not every scene of the game; the number of sublevels is under each module.

Every percentage is out of the participants who STARTED the game (played at least one analysed sublevel), not the whole cohort.

REACHED = played at least one of the module's analysed sublevels; COMPLETED = played all of them; SUBLEVELS PLAYED = average share of them played.

Source: dss_secrets/pilot_summary/game_scores_summary.csv (functionalities/offline/build_game_scores_csv.py).
Every percentage is out of the 700 participants who started the game (played at least one analysed sublevel), not the whole cohort. A module is the set of its sublevels analysed by the DSS, chosen by the clinicians, not every scene of the game. Reached = played at least one of them; Completed = played all of them; Sublevels played = average share of them played.
