ⓘ About this view
The participant's moods and PROMs on one page.

MOOD: the average and spread of every mood over the whole period, sleep quality, and the selected mood day by day against the cohort.

PROMs (patient-reported outcome measures): the questionnaire sessions, newest first, with the mood around each session.

MOOD SCALE: every mood is on a -3 to +3 valence scale (answers 1-7): -3 = negative valence, +3 = positive valence. Negative moods (anxious, irritated, sad, insecure, lonely, stressed) are reversed, so higher is always better: -3 on "anxious" means very anxious, +3 not anxious at all.

WARNINGS ("!" button): fewer than 10 days with ESM answers, or a pause of more than 7 days between two days with answers.

Mood

Cohort reference Reference = participants of the same country, gender as the selected participant. All moods: −3 = negative valence, +3 = positive valence; negative moods are reversed.

ⓘ About this view
Each tile is one mood over every ESM answer of the participant: the large number is the average, the bar under it shows where the average sits between -3 (negative valence) and +3 (positive valence), and the white band its spread (+/- 1 standard deviation).

Positive moods: higher = more of the mood. Negative moods are reversed: higher = less of the mood.

SLEEP: one answer per night (the sleep question of the morning). A night is negative below neutral (answers 1-3). "Runs of more than 3 negative nights" counts stretches of at least 4 consecutive nights all negative; a night without an answer interrupts a run.

ⓘ About this view
The selected mood day by day, on the -3 (negative valence) to +3 (positive valence) scale.

- Dots: the daily average (hover for the single answers of that day).

- Solid line and shaded band: the weekly average (Monday to Sunday) and its standard error.

- Grey dashed line and hatched band: the cohort average and its typical range, for the reference group picked above the tiles (whole cohort, or the participants of the same country / gender / age group).

- Dotted vertical lines: Mondays. Background shading: weekdays, lighter on Monday to darker on Sunday.

- Diamonds under the scale: days with diary entries.

OPTIONAL (click in the legend to show or hide): the daily standard deviation as error bars, a smoothed trend, the weekday shading.

Use the slider under the chart to zoom on a period.
