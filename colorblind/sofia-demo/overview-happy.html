ⓘ About this view
Who is in the cohort and how it is doing.

THE COHORT: pick a variable (an ESM mood item, mood valence, sleep quality or an emotional wellbeing factor) with the selector above the tiles; the map of Europe, the tree map and the distribution all show that variable.

MOOD SCALE: every mood is on a -3 to +3 valence scale (answers 1-7): -3 = negative valence, +3 = positive valence. Negative moods (anxious, irritated, sad, insecure, lonely, stressed) are reversed, so higher is always better: -3 on "anxious" means very anxious, +3 not anxious at all.

THE GAME: completion and points module by module, over the participants who started the game.

The selected participant is highlighted in the distribution and their country is outlined on the map.

Distribution by country — happy

ⓘ About this view
DISTRIBUTION BY COUNTRY:

One violin per country with every participant's average of the selected variable; the violin is colored by the country's average (same colors as the map and the tree map). Inside: a box (median and quartiles) and the mean (line). Each dot is a participant (shape and color = gender); the large star is the selected participant.

For moods, the lower half (below 0) is negative valence and the upper half positive valence; negative moods are reversed.

SUGGESTED INTERACTION:

- Hover on a violin for the country's mean, median and n; on a dot for the participant's gender, age group and value.

- Click a gender in the legend to hide or show it.

Points and completion by module

ⓘ About this view
The game over the cohort, module by module. A module here means the sublevels of that module the DSS analyses (the clinicians' sheet), not every scene of the game; the number of sublevels is under each module.

Every percentage is out of the participants who STARTED the game (played at least one analysed sublevel), not the whole cohort.

TOP: points awarded as a share of the points possible, in total and in Thoughts and Behaviours: bar = mean, line = standard deviation, over the participants who played that part of the module (hover for n).

BOTTOM: REACHED = played at least one of the module's analysed sublevels; COMPLETED = played all of them; diamond = average share of them played.

The numbers are in the table below the chart. Source: dss_secrets/pilot_summary/game_scores_summary.csv (functionalities/offline/build_game_scores_csv.py).
