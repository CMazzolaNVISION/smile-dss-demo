ⓘ About this view
Game sublevels selected by the clinicians for the analysis of game scores against mood. The page opens on the whole game (every module), then shows one module at a time (module 1 by default; change it in the "Module" section).

DEFINITIONS:

- Points: points awarded / points possible, computed with the rule written in the sublevel table. Only the first completion of an exercise awards points; replays report the same value. Percentages only count sublevels with points logged.

- Decision time: total time to decide across the sublevel's tasks and mini tasks, from the decision latency (DurationOfSelection) or the dialog decision time (DurationOfInteraction) where the game logs it, otherwise from the time on task (Playtime). Replays included.

- Attempts: number of times the sublevel's tasks and mini tasks were played, replays included.

- Healthy thinking score: (points awarded² / points possible) / (decision time × attempts). Shown as n/a when the game logs no decision time or no points for the sublevel.

- ESM: mood valence is the mean of "happy" and reversed "sad" (1-7, higher = better); anxiety and stress are the "anxious" and "stressed" items REVERSED (8 - answer, so higher = better), as everywhere in the DSS: + = positive valence, - = negative valence. The CSV download keeps the raw answers, in the clinicians' layout. For a sublevel, the closest entry before and after it; for a module, the closest entry before its first sublevel and after its last one; always within 2 days.
